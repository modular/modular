---
title: The value lifecycle
sidebar_position: 1
description: >
  Follow Mojo values from creation and initialization through ownership
  transfer and destruction, including the simpler lifecycle of trivial types.
---

A Mojo value has a beginning and an end. Mojo creates and initializes the
value before you use it, then destroys it when it's no longer needed. Along
the way, the value may be copied, moved, or transferred to another owner.

Lifecycle methods let you customize how your types create values, manage
their state, and release resources. Mojo handles much of this
automatically, while giving you control when your types need it. Register
and trivial types simplify the lifecycle by eliminating operations they
don't need.
